01. Commitment to Client Privacy & Discretion

Welcome to ST Watches Pakistan ("we," "our," or "us"). As a bespoke horological brand delivering premium luxury and precision timepieces throughout Pakistan, we respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy governs your use of our web boutique (stwatches.pk), our WhatsApp order concierge, and our direct customer assistance services. By visiting our website or ordering timepieces via Cash on Delivery (COD) or WhatsApp, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

Our Core Guarantee: Zero Third-Party Monetization

We strictly do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize your contact or purchase details to marketing brokers, ad agencies, or unauthorized third parties under any circumstance.

02. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is strictly essential to process your timepiece orders, fulfill safe doorstep delivery, and provide personalized horological customer support:

  • Delivery Coordinates: Your Full Name, Street Address, Apartment/House/Plaza number, Area/Sector, City (e.g., Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta, and all other cities in Pakistan), and Postal Code.
  • Direct Contact Data: Active Phone Number / WhatsApp Mobile Number (used strictly by our team and courier dispatch riders to contact you for address confirmation and parcel handover).
  • Electronic Communication: Email Address (used for sending formal order receipts, dispatch tracking links, and optional Collector's Circle newsletters).
  • Order History & Warranty Data: Watch model, quantity, serial or reference number, order ID, purchase date, and official 1-Year Movement Warranty registration.
  • Customer Correspondence: Inquiries, strap adjustments, or after-sales servicing requests communicated via WhatsApp, phone, or website contact forms.

03. How We Utilize Your Information

Your information is applied solely towards the following authentic business operations:

  • Order Verification & COD Booking: Validating your order via phone or WhatsApp prior to booking the sealed parcel with Pakistan's premier courier networks.
  • Courier Transit & Doorstep Delivery: Generating the shipping air-waybill (AWB) to guarantee timely transit and correct recipient verification.
  • Warranty Registration: Logging your unique timepiece reference in our customer archive so you can claim warranty repairs or movement service without hassle.
  • Customer Support & Concierge Care: Answering sizing questions, providing technical movement specifications, or processing exchange requests.
  • Curated Collector Previews: Sending private release updates if you have opted in to our newsletter or WhatsApp VIP list.

04. Payment & Cash on Delivery (COD) Protection

We prioritize utmost financial security for our clientele across Pakistan:

  • Cash on Delivery (COD): For maximum buyer peace of mind, you do not need to provide credit card numbers on our website. You simply inspect the sealed box and pay the delivery rider in cash upon receiving your timepiece.
  • Direct Bank Transfers: If you select direct bank deposit, transfers are executed directly from your own secure mobile banking application to our verified business account. We never ask for or store your ATM PIN, online banking passwords, or card CVVs.
  • Zero Digital Card Storage: Our web servers do not hold, process, or archive your debit/credit card records.

05. Courier Logistics & Trusted Service Partners

To successfully transport luxury timepieces across Pakistan, we collaborate exclusively with reputable, insured, and verified logistics partners (including TCS, Trax, Leopard Courier, and M&P).

We share only the minimal details necessary for physical parcel dispatch:

  • Customer Name, Delivery Address, and Active Contact Phone.
  • Parcel invoice amount (Cash on Delivery total) to be collected by the courier rider upon delivery.
  • Specific delivery notes (e.g. "Call before arrival", "Deliver after 2 PM").

These courier carriers are bound by strict contractual confidentiality and are legally prohibited from utilizing your contact details for any extraneous purpose.

06. Newsletter & WhatsApp Communications

When you enter your email in our newsletter subscription form or initiate a chat via our WhatsApp order button:

  • You will receive VIP notifications regarding limited timepiece drops, seasonal reductions, or horological articles.
  • Instant Opt-Out: You may unsubscribe from email broadcasts at any time with a single click, or reply "STOP" to any WhatsApp promotional broadcast to be removed immediately.

07. Data Security & Storage Standards

We implement industry-standard technical safeguards to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, accidental alteration, or disclosure:

  • SSL/TLS 256-Bit Encryption: All browsing and checkout data transmitted through our web storefront is encrypted end-to-end.
  • Restricted Personnel Access: Only authorized order processing managers and logistics staff have access to order records for fulfillment purposes.
  • Browser Storage: Items added to your cart are retained locally in your web browser (via HTML5 local storage) for your convenience during checkout and do not track your activity across other websites.

08. Your Rights & Privacy Concierge Contact

As a valued ST Watches patron, you have full authority over your data. You may at any time:

  • Request a full transcript of your past orders and personal contact data on file.
  • Request immediate correction or updating of your delivery address and phone number.
  • Request complete erasure and deletion of your profile and records from our customer database.
